The brief

Flight training is a path, not a booking

Most student pilots never finish. Progress resets when they change cities, schools, or instructors, and the tools around training treat each lesson as an isolated appointment instead of a certificate journey.

AviPrep needed a product that felt like a school without the lock-in: find certified instructors nationwide, see cost and timeline before the first lesson, and keep hours, milestones, and recaps tied to the student, not the school.

Approach

Design the journey, then ship the system

I owned product design and frontend end to end: research and flows, landing pages, interaction design, and a reusable UI system in React and TypeScript that could grow with new surfaces (DPE finder, cost calculator, lesson recaps, training record) without restarting the visual language each time.

  • Clarity before polish

    Hero and onboarding had one job: make the path to certificate obvious. Instructor search, DPE lookup, and “Get Started” sit in a hierarchy that mirrors how students actually decide.

  • Reusable UI, not one-off screens

    Components and patterns were built to stretch across marketing and product surfaces so new features (path planner, AI ground-lesson recaps, ADS-B flight summaries) landed in the same system.

  • Trust for a high-stakes category

    Dark, calm UI with precise type and map-led visuals. Aviation students need confidence more than novelty; the interface stays out of the way of the decision.

The work

From first visit to a portable training record

Key surfaces I designed and built include the marketing site and core product flows: instructor discovery, personalized training path and cost estimates, Designated Pilot Examiner lookup, lesson recaps with ACS-tagged takeaways, and a training record that travels with the student across CFIs and cities.

  • 01

    Landing & conversion: structured the homepage around the certificate journey—profile, book, fly—so first-time visitors understand the product before they hit a form.

  • 02

    Path & cost clarity: turned opaque flight-school pricing into a planner students can run themselves: goal, aircraft, pace, estimated total, and months to checkride.

  • 03

    After every lesson: ground lessons become AI recaps; flights surface as ADS-B track summaries so progress is visible, not buried in a binder.
